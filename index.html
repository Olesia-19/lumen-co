Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

Does the initial consultation cost anything?+
No — your first consultation is complimentary and comes with no obligation to book treatment.
What happens during the consultation?+
We begin with a conversation about your goals, then assess your skin and facial structure. From there, we explain which options — if any — may be appropriate, and what to expect. Consultations are unhurried, and you leave with clear information before deciding whether to proceed.
Will I be pressured to book treatment?+
No. The consultation is a conversation, not a sales appointment. We will not recommend a treatment you do not need, and there is no obligation to book. If treatment is not the right next step, we will say so.
Is there any downtime?+
Most treatments have little to no downtime. Your practitioner will walk you through exactly what to expect for your specific plan.
How do I know what treatment is right for me?+
That's what the consultation is for. We assess your goals and skin first, then walk you through the treatment options best suited to what you're looking for.
How do you approach natural-looking results?+
We aim to enhance what is already there — not replace it. That means conservative dosing, respect for natural expression, and a refusal to chase trends that would look overdone. The goal is a refreshed result that still reads as you.
What qualifications does the practitioner have?+
Treatments are performed by Dr. Elena Marsh, a board-certified nurse practitioner and the founder of Lumen & Co. She brings twelve years of clinical experience before specialising in facial aesthetics. That medical background informs how consultations and treatment decisions are approached.
How is the final treatment price determined?+
Starting prices are listed on this page. The final price depends on the treatment, the areas treated, the amount of product required where applicable, and your individual assessment. Exact pricing is confirmed during your consultation, based on your goals.
What's your safety protocol?+
All treatments are performed by a board-certified practitioner in a licensed medical facility, following standard clinical safety and sterilization procedures.
Do you accept walk-ins?+
An in-person consultation is required before any first treatment. You can book your consultation online any time.
